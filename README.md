One image summing up the world's news of Wednesday 25 November 2026, drawn as
a newspaper front page: the lead story is a Houthi drone hitting Riyadh's
tallest tower, wounding 11 — the second strike on the landmark in a week — with
a drawn skyline of the PIF tower; two more stories cover the cyberattack on the
CIA and the Director of National Intelligence's unclassified system, and the US
jury convicting Huawei on 10 of 12 counts; three cards cover Zelenskyy
confirming Ukraine's strike on the Russian ship Sparta II, Trump giving
Giuliani the Medal of Freedom and pardoning turkeys Freedom and Feathers, and
England crushing Australia by eight wickets in the 2nd T20 — plus a Sarkozy
trial, the BJP–SAD reunion, the WHO evacuation in DR Congo, and Nvidia's Huang
returning as chief executive in the also-today strip. Every headline is set
from the day's reporting; the image is drawn programmatically, so no text can
garble.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Deterministic, no network, standard library
only.
